What is CodexaCoin?

CodexaCoin (ticker CAC) is a Proof-of-Stake (PoS) cryptocurrency, forked from the Blackcoin More codebase and rebuilt with its own consensus parameters, staking-reward formula, and full application stack — desktop wallet, mobile wallet, block explorer, and a custodial staking service.

Instead of ongoing energy-intensive mining, new blocks after the initial founder-premine window are produced by staking: coin holders who keep their wallet online periodically "mint" new blocks in proportion to the coins they hold, and are rewarded for it. There's no ongoing hardware arms race and no perpetual electricity cost per block.

The project is open source under the MIT license, inheriting its lineage from Bitcoin Core, Peercoin, and Blackcoin.
